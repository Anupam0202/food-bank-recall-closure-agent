Design intent

Closure—not another alert.

This Taskmaster workflow converts a trusted source record into reversible holds, accountable partner work, and portable proof of internal completion.

What Gemini does

  • Extracts inconsistent recall identifiers into a validated schema.
  • Reads unclear package evidence for human review.
  • Summarizes unresolved operational actions through a real ADK coordinator.

What deterministic controls do

  • Authorize quarantine holds only for exact identifiers.
  • Prevent duplicate work and illegal state transitions.
  • Require human resolution and acknowledgement before closure.
Winning differentiator

From signal to independently verifiable evidence

Bounded autonomy

The model interprets; policy authorizes.

Operational closure

Partner work remains visible until acknowledged.

Tamper evidence

Portable packs hash every member and chain ordered audit events.

Cost-aware cloud

Scale-to-zero deployment and a one-instance default protect the demo budget.

!
Not a regulator system

The application never declares food safe, orders disposal, publishes public alerts, or changes official recall status. Free-tier Gemini usage should not receive confidential data without an approved data-handling review.
